**Nothing on our servers.** Skill Recorder is a Chrome extension that records browser activity locally and saves the result into your own browser's IndexedDB and your local Downloads folder. We do not operate a backend that ingests your recordings.

## What the website collects

This marketing website may use [PostHog](https://posthog.com) to capture anonymous page-view events when an opt-in cookie banner is accepted. No personal data is sent without consent.

## What the extension transmits

The extension does not transmit any of your recordings, action logs, or distilled SKILL.md files to any remote server. The cloud-sync feature in the roadmap is opt-in and will be documented separately when shipped.

## What you control

All recordings live in `chrome.storage` / IndexedDB on your machine. Clearing the extension's data via `chrome://extensions` deletes everything immediately. No remote copy exists.

## Third parties

- **Cloudflare Pages** hosts this site and may log standard HTTP metadata for security purposes.
- **PostHog** (optional, EU-hosted) collects anonymous product analytics when the cookie banner is accepted.

No other third parties receive your data.
